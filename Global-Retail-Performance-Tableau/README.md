---

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project analyzes a global retail dataset to evaluate business performance across countries, product categories, customer segments, and sales channels. The objective was to uncover trends in sales and profitability, identify inefficiencies, and build an interactive dashboard to support strategic decision-making.

The final output is a **fully interactive Tableau dashboard** designed for business stakeholders to explore performance dynamically.

---

## 📊 Dataset Summary  
- Transactional retail dataset  
- Multi-country and multi-city coverage  
- Includes product categories and sub-categories  

**Key Fields:**
- Order Date  
- Country, City  
- Category, Sub-Category  
- Segment  
- Sales Channel (Online/Offline)  
- Sales, Quantity, Discount, Profit  

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation & Feature Engineering  

The following calculated fields were created:

- **Profit Ratio** → `Profit / Sales`  
- **Discount Level** → Categorized into No, Low, Medium, High  
- **Average Sales per Unit** → `SUM(Sales) / SUM(Quantity)`  

These transformations enabled deeper analysis of profitability, pricing strategy, and product efficiency.

## 📈 Analytical Techniques Used  

### Business Analysis
- Time-series trend analysis  
- Profitability analysis  
- Efficiency analysis  
- Comparative analysis (category, segment, channel)  

### Tableau Features
- Calculated fields  
- Aggregations (SUM, AVG)  
- Running total (cumulative sales)  
- Interactive filters  
- Context filter for time-based refinement  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features  

- 📈 **Sales Trend Analysis** (time-series with running total)  
- 📊 **Category Performance** (sales vs profit comparison)  
- 🔄 **Sales Channel Comparison** (Online vs Offline)  
- 💸 **Discount Impact Analysis** (discount vs profitability)  

### 🎛️ Interactive Filters:
- Country  
- Segment  
- Sales Channel  
- Date (context filter applied)  

Clean, structured layout designed for executive-level insights.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights  

- The business shows **steady growth over time**, indicating stable performance  
- High-revenue categories do not always generate high profit, highlighting **margin inefficiencies**  
- Higher discount levels are strongly associated with **lower or negative profitability**  
- The **offline sales channel slightly outperforms online** in both revenue and profit  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations  

- Optimize discount strategies to prevent profit erosion  
- Improve margins in underperforming categories  
- Invest strategically in high-performing sales channels  
- Balance revenue growth with profitability
